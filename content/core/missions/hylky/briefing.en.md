# Orders

Our reconnaissance plane came down this morning behind the enemy line east of Povenets. The pilot, **Lieutenant
Saarinen**, is alive by his last radio call but cannot move. He has a **map case** that must not fall into enemy hands.

**Find the wreck** in the area marked on the map and **bring Saarinen** and his map case back to the start area.
The ahkio man's sled pulls a wounded man quickly.

There is an enemy post on the knoll on the lake's south shore, with a field telephone; the wire runs east. If the post is
alarmed and gets through, their reserve comes. The enemy saw the plane come down: expect a search party from the east.

Half a metre of snow, and the evening is drawing in. Every man leaves tracks.
