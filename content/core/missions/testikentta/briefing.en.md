# Testbed

A scene for development and testing, not a mission to play. Everything is in view and happens at once:

- a searched body (a tick), an unsearched one (a haversack) and a wounded Soviet on the slope;
- three Soviets in the foxholes on the knoll, 45 m ahead;
- in your own squad a wounded man and a fallen one, for the cards.

Double click a body to search it, double click an enemy to attack. K changes the card size, O the outlines.
