# Orders

There is an enemy post on the knoll, with a camp behind it. The post has a **field telephone**; its wire runs north-east
(the dashed line on the map). If the post is alarmed and gets through, their reserve comes. **Cut the wire** (right
click beside it) and the call does not get through.

The sentries are calm: slow to notice and slow to fire. A shot, a shout or a flare wakes the rest. A patrol walks the
slope.

**Find the cache** west of the knoll and **get back**. After four minutes an enemy search party sets out for the same
area.
