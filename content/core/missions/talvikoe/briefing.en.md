# Orders

February dusk, 40 cm of snow. Private Rinne was left wounded in the woods west of the knoll. **Find him** and **bring
him back** to the start area. Aho's ahkio pulls him faster than carrying.

Men on skis are quick; a man on foot sinks into the snow. Every man leaves tracks: the enemy notices them, and they can
be followed. At dusk little is seen; a flare lights the ground.
